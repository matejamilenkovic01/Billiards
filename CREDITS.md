## Generated
The ball-roll and room-ambience loops are synthesised by `Assets/_Scripts/Editor/Builders/GeneratedAudio.cs` (placeholders).
Real billiard recordings dropped into `_kenney_downloads/billiard_sfx/` (names in the gameplay doc, P2) replace the
placeholders on the next Systems Prefab build (`SystemsPrefabBuilder.Sounds`).

Ball textures and icons, cloth and wood, cue art, coin / hand / eye icons, the coach-mark hand's shadow, the rank badge and checkpoint flag, the CPU
characters' avatars (a disc in the division's colour and a silhouette; initials are drawn over them), the five venues'
wallpaper and carpet textures and UI shapes are generated by `Assets/_Scripts/Editor/Builders/GeneratedArtBuilder.cs`.
The five rooms and their neon signs (Rubik) come from `RoomPrefabBuilder`; the venue thumbnails are
renders of those rooms (`MatchSceneBuilder.BakeVenues`). Portraits dropped into `_kenney_downloads/portraits/<id>.png`
(256×256) replace the generated avatars on the next Generated Art build.
